# Technical GRC + AI Governance Portfolio

Hi, I'm Dhanvi Acharya. This repository is where I collect what I'm learning about **governance, risk and compliance (GRC)** from a technical point of view, with a growing focus on **governing AI systems, especially autonomous agents**.

Most GRC material stops at the policy. I'm interested in the next question: *what actually happens in the system when a control is in place, and how would you prove it?* A framework says "monitor your systems". A technical GRC person can say which logs, which detection rule, and which evidence satisfy that line. That gap is what I'm trying to learn to close.

> **Status:** work in progress. This is a learning portfolio, not professional audit or legal advice. Where I'm interpreting a framework rather than quoting it, I say so.

## Topics I cover

- **Cloud security and compliance foundations:** shared responsibility, Zero Trust, identity, and the Microsoft security and compliance stack (I'm currently studying for the SC-900 exam)
- **AI governance:** agentic AI risk, non-human identities, the OWASP Agentic Top 10, and how NIST AI RMF, ISO/IEC 27001, SOC 2 and the EU AI Act relate to them
- **Security operations as evidence:** SIEM and SOAR, logging and monitoring, and incident response mapped to audit requirements
- **Regional regulation:** how cyber and data governance is layered in India and how it applies to businesses serving foreign clients

## How I write these notes

- I read the primary source where I can and link it.
- I separate **what a framework says** from **my interpretation** of what it means in practice.
- I mark anything I haven't been able to verify, and I'd rather leave a gap than guess.
- If something is out of date or wrong, please tell me.
